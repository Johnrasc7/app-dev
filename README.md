## Interstellar
*Directed by Christopher Nolan*

> "Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."

**Interstellar** is a visually stunning masterpiece that explores the limits of human endurance and the mind-bending realities of astrophysics, specifically time dilation and gravity.

### Why It's Amazing for Me:
*   **Scientific Accuracy:** The depiction of the black hole, *Gargantua*, was so accurate that it led to published scientific papers and also because of that I felt more intereseted in science.
*   **The Music:** Hans Zimmer's organ-heavy soundtrack is legendary and builds incredible tension.
*   **Story:** Beneath the science stuff, it is fundamentally a story about love and the bond between a father and his daughter.

## Project Hail Mary
*Written by Andy Weir*

This novel is a brilliant mix of isolation, deep-space exploration, and using the scientific method to survive impossible odds. 

### The Main Duo
| Character | Species | Expertise |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ryland Grace** | Human | Middle school science teacher / Astronaut |
| **Rocky** | Eridian | Master engineer / Loyal friend |

**The Key Concept:** The plot revolves around a mysterious, energy-eating microbe called `Astrophage` that threatens to dim the sun and freeze Earth. 

### Why It's Amazing for Me:
*   **Science Stuff:** The depiction and visualization of tech in this movie are somewhat felt so real to me, and probably it is real.
*   **Story:** It revolves around research in order to make humanity survive longer, it also revolves around the friendship of the protagonist and a extraterrestrial being.

## Dr. Stone
*Created by Riichiro Inagaki & Boichi*

Imagine all of humanity is suddenly turned to stone for 3,700 years. Then, a brilliant, science-obsessed teenager wakes up and decides to rebuild civilization from scratch.

### Senku's Roadmap to Civilization:
1.  **Survive:** Secure food, shelter, and clothing in a dangerous, overgrown stone-age world.
2.  **Revive:** Formulate the revival fluid (a mixture of nitric acid and ethanol) to un-petrify humanity.
3.  **Innovate:** Reinvent everything from soap and antibiotics to electricity, ramen, and cell phones.

### Why It's Amazing for Me:
*   **Science Stuff:** There are a lot of real things that is invented through the stuff that they show in that anime/manga. I loved that they do something like that.
*   **Story:** It revolves around reviving humanity through revival fluid while having to face challenges from other people or even unknown object.
